Rust Base Prototyper – controls

First-person (default): click the 3D view to capture the mouse. W A S D move, mouse look, Space up, Shift down, Ctrl hold for faster. Esc releases mouse.
Overview: Tab (or View button) toggles orbit view: left-drag rotate, right-drag pan, wheel zoom, click to place.
Build: pick a piece with 1–9,0, Q/E or mouse wheel (FP) or the hotbar. Ghost is green = valid, red = blocked. Left click place, R rotate, Right click or X remove the piece you aim at.
Hammer: H toggles hammer mode – left click a piece to set it to the selected tier. T cycles tier (Twig → Wood → Stone → Metal → Armored).
Snapping: first foundation snaps to a 3 m grid on the ground; more foundations snap to foundation edges. Walls / doorways / windows / frames snap to foundation or floor edges (or on top of walls). Floors & roofs snap onto full wall tops (on the side you're looking from) or next to other floors. Stairs go in a square foundation/floor. Doors go in doorways; the garage door (sheet metal only, one wall wide, like real Rust) goes in a wall frame – tap it or aim + F to roll it up/down; bars/embrasures in window frames. Deployables (TC, boxes, turrets, benches, electrical…) don't use tiers: floor items go on foundation/floor tops (or the ground), wall items (signs, shelves, drop box, switches…) stick to wall faces, the ceiling light goes on a floor's underside. C or the tabs switch category; number keys pick within the category. Locks: pick Key Lock or Code Lock and click/tap a door or garage door to attach (one per door); the remove tool on a locked door takes the lock off first.
Stories: ]/PageUp next story, [/PageDown previous (0 = All), V toggles Only-this-story / Ghost-lower-stories. With a story selected, other stories are hidden (or faint) and you aim at a build plane at that story's floor height, so you can lay floors there even before walls exist.
Weapons: G (or Weapons button) toggles weapons mode – building is disabled while it's on. Click / hold to fire (phone: hold FIRE), R reload, B or middle-click cycles ammo type (reloads into it), C/tabs switch weapon group, wheel/number keys pick a gun. Bullets chip building HP, rockets/C4 can destroy pieces (approximate, not exact). Reset damage restores everything; damage is never saved in blueprints. C4/satchel stick where you aim within 5 m and blow after 3 s (shortened).
Other: Ctrl+Z / U undo. Blueprints are saved in this browser (localStorage); export JSON or copy a share link to move them.
Phone: everything aims at the center crosshair. Left joystick moves, drag to look (overview: drag orbits), tap anywhere or hit PLACE to place/remove/hammer/lock at the crosshair, Up/Down buttons fly, Remove toggles delete-on-tap.
